  • Application guidance: Help sellers understand registration fields, prepare their own documents, and respond to verification requests using accurate information.
  • Account configuration: Assist with the seller profile, shipping and fulfilment preferences, return settings, and other launch settings within the agreed scope.
  • Launch preparation: Create a checklist for the first product listings, inventory readiness, and operational responsibilities before sales begin.

What you receive

  • Document and information checklist
  • Registration and verification progress summary
  • Account configuration and launch checklist

How it helps your business

Our approach

Business discussion → document readiness review → guided registration and verification follow-up → configuration and launch handover.

Initial document guidance can be provided without direct account access. Configuration work is carried out only after the seller grants the relevant permissions through Amazon’s approved authorization process.

Advertising Optimization

Our Advertising Optimization service helps Amazon.in sellers plan, manage, and improve advertising campaigns around their products, available budget, and business objectives.   • Search-term and targeting reviews: Evaluate search terms and target performance to identify relevant opportunities, refine targeting, and add negative keywords or targets where appropriate.
  • Bid, placement, and budget management: Review bids and placement settings, adjust them within the agreed scope, and allocate campaign budgets according to performance and seller-approved spending limits.

  • Make campaign decisions using performance data
